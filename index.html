8-FACTOR MODEL
Research-backed composite scoring
Factor 1 — Primary Power
Exit Velocity + Barrel%
Barrel% is the single strongest Statcast predictor of future HRs. ~85% of all HRs are barrels. EV 95+ mph is required threshold. Elite: EV 112+ / Barrel 15%+.
Weight: 22%
Factor 2 — Elevation
Launch Angle + Sweet Spot%
Optimal HR launch angle is 25–35°. Sweet Spot% (8-32° range) measures consistency. Pull Air% reveals pull-field power generation — most HRs are pulled.
Weight: 18%
Factor 3 — Bat Speed
Bat Speed + Fast Swing%
Bat speed directly drives exit velocity. Every mph of bat speed adds ~1.2 mph of EV. Elite bat speed (75+ mph avg) with high hard-hit% = premium dinger profile.
Weight: 12%
Factor 4 — Pitcher Vulnerability
HR/9 + Barrel% Allowed
Pitchers above 1.4 HR/9 are high-leverage targets. Barrel% allowed and hard-hit% give the true quality of contact surrendered — not just results.
Weight: 20%
Factor 5 — Platoon
Batter/Pitcher Handedness
Handedness splits vs specific pitcher side (LHB vs RHP, RHB vs LHP are favored matchups). Pitcher's HR splits by batter hand are loaded directly from CSV files.
Weight: 10%
Factor 6 — Park Factor
HR Park Index (2025–26)
UNIQLO Field (LAD) leads all parks at 135 HR factor. Coors, Camden, GABP are also strong. Oracle/PNC/Comerica suppress. Factor adjusts composite score ±15 pts.
Weight: 10%
Factor 7 — Hot Streak
Recent HR Rate + Distance
Avg distance on fly balls indicates current power. HR/FB rate over recent games shows who's elevating well right now. Form matters — momentum is real.
Weight: 5%
Factor 8 — HR/FB Ratio
Power Conversion Rate
% of fly balls that leave the yard. League avg ~12%. 18%+ = legitimate power. Below 10% without supporting metrics suggests regression incoming — or a prime buy-low spot.
Weight: 3%
